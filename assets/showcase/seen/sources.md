Quiet natural daylight, beautiful real paper fibers, soft cast shadows, calm precise product photography, close crop on these two paper objects, sophisticated restrained material realism, grounded in a real everyday workspace. No people, hands, pens, extra decorative objects, plants, logos, watermark or letters anywhere. Absolutely no writing on the sticky note, no words on the book or monitor. The two visible pieces of paper are the emotional focal points. The image will be a supporting illustration under a Chinese headline that is added separately; generate no typography.
